F.P.Journe Chronomètre À Résonance Ref. RN Souveraine Platinum Grey Dial Full Set Unpolished

F.P.Journe has become one of the most sought after and beloved independent watchmakers. Mr. Journe is one of the most significant watchmakers of the past century. F.P.Journe graduated from the Paris School of Watchmaking in 1976 and would go on to establish his manufacture in 1999 launching his Subscription Model of 20 examples purchased by close friends. The Chronomètre à Résonance is arguably his most iconic model.

François-Paul Journe took inspiration for the Chronomètre À Résonance from pendulum clocks featuring two complete movements and independent precision escapements. Each of these pendulums exchanged energy with a synchronized natural resonance, neutralizing external vibrations. The power reserve indicator is located at twelve o’clock and uses the format seen in marine chronometers.

This is an early 2nd generation example of the RN (Resonance Nouvelle) in platinum sold in 2005. The watch is powered by the rare 18k rose gold calibre 1499.2. The rose gold calibre 1499.2 was produced for a short period after Journe transitioned from the rhodium-plated brass movement. These are seen from circa 2005-2006. Soon after, the 1499.2 was replaced by the calibre 1499.3.

This Chronomètre à Résonance reference RN is made of platinum. It measures 40mm in diameter. The case appears to be unpolished. The dial is made of white gold. It is a stunning and clean look against the platinum case. It is paired on an original F.P.Journe leather strap and platinum buckle.

The Chronomètre À Résonance reference RN would remain in production until 2009 and remains an icon in independent watchmaking and one of the most desirable wristwatches on the planet.

We do not know the service history, but the watch is running well. It comes full set with a box and card.
